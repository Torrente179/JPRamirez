This is a private app for my training, food and groceries. It's built around how I actually train and eat: a training week I can change, macro targets that depend on the type of day, and grocery lists for the stores I shop at.

## What it does

- **Four sections**: Today, Plan (the training week), Food (meals) and Week (the review)
- **Training models** I can switch between, with 2-day or 3-day splits and macro targets for each type of day
- **Quick-add meals**, including ones I've defined myself
- A **grocery catalog** for Mercadona and HiperDino, scored on price against protein, so I know what to buy, where and why
- A **weekly review** with a shopping list and a few rule-based suggestions
- Magic-link sign-in through Supabase, and a one-time import from the static dashboard it replaced

## Build notes

It's Next.js 16 with React Server Components and Supabase (magic links, Postgres and row-level security). The part I care most about is the domain layer. The training rules, macro maths and grocery scoring are plain functions in `lib/domain/`, all tested with Vitest, and the UI sits on top of them.

## Status

It's a private beta that runs my training week. I might turn it into a product later, but I wanted the numbers right first.
