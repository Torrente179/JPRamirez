I needed to track my hours across clients and turn them into invoices without paying for another tool, so I built one. It's what I use for my freelance work.

## What it does

- A **weekly calendar** with tasks stored in UTC, drag and drop, and timers you can pause
- **Invoices** that go from draft to final. An invoice only gets its number once it's finalized
- **Exports** to PDF, CSV and PNG
- **Monthly reports**, with a search for hours by client and Recharts dashboards
- Sign-in with email and password, Google, Apple or a passkey (WebAuthn)
- A one-time import from the older localStorage version of the app

## Build notes

The client is React 19 and Vite. Supabase handles auth and Postgres, with row-level security on the data. PDFs are generated in the browser with jsPDF and html2canvas, so making an invoice doesn't need a server.

Time is stored in UTC and shown in whatever timezone you're in, so the hours always add up.

## Status

It's live at [jptimetracker.vercel.app](https://jptimetracker.vercel.app) and I use it for my own work.
