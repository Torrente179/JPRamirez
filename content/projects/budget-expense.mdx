This is the finance app I wanted for myself. English and Spanish. Envelopes, investments, giving, and a few currencies.

## What it does

- **Dashboard** with balance, monthly spend, and how those changed
- **Envelope budgeting** with a progress bar per pool
- **Movements** with full history and a calendar you can click into
- **Investments and net worth** next to day-to-day spending
- **Imports**: CSV in the app, plus Santander statements
- **Multi-currency** (20 currencies, EUR as the base) with conversion
- Spending charts by category, via Recharts

## Build notes

Next.js 16, React Server Components, Supabase (auth, Postgres, row-level security), Tailwind CSS v4, Base UI. Soft neutrals, `tabular-nums` on every number, green for up, rose for down. Phone gets a bottom nav. Desktop gets a sidebar.

## Status

Live, and I'm still adding to it. Lately: calendar, transfers, net worth, CSV import.
